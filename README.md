# Sequence-Based Discovery and Prioritization of Novel AAV Capsids

## Project Overview

This project analyzes capsid sequence-identity data from the MADCAP study
(Lyashenko et al., 2026) to investigate sequence diversity among newly
identified adeno-associated virus (AAV) capsids.

The analysis compares 139 MADCAP-discovered capsids with 162 previously
known natural isolates and calculates metrics including nearest-known
sequence identity, overall similarity, and sequence divergence.

The broader goal is to explore how computational sequence analysis can
help prioritize viral capsids for additional structural and experimental
characterization.

## Dataset

Primary dataset:

MADCAP Supplementary Table S5 — Identity matrix of all capsids used in
the study.

Source:
Lyashenko et al. (2026), Journal of Virology.
"Metagenomic AAV Discovery and Characterization Pipeline (MADCAP):
isolation of novel nAb-naive AAV capsids from metagenomic data."
